TECHNICAL ANALYSIS OF STRATEGY
================================================================================

HOW BOLLINGER BAND SQUEEZE WORKS:
  1. Monitor 20-period Bollinger Bands (SMA ± 2 std dev)
  2. Detect "squeeze" when bandwidth < 4% (tight bands = low volatility)
  3. Wait for price to break out of bands
  4. Enter trade in direction of breakout
  5. Stop loss = the SMA (mean)
  6. Take profit = entry + 2.5x (entry - SMA)

WHAT THE DATA SHOWS:
  - Signal Generation: 96 signals in 50 days ≈ 2 signals/day (frequent)
  - Trade Efficiency: Only 35% of signals become profitable trades
  - Win Rate: 29.4% means ~7 losses for every 2-3 wins
  - Average Loss: ~$1,000 per losing trade
  - Average Win: ~$1,900 per winning trade
  - Risk vs Reward: Winners are ~1.9x larger than losers (good!)

PROFITABILITY ANALYSIS:
  Current: 10 wins × $1,900 = $19,000 gross profits
           24 losses × $1,000 = $24,000 gross losses
           Net = -$5,000 (actual: -$4,067)
