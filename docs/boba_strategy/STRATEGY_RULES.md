# Strategy Rules: Boba Trades (Supply & Demand Reversals)

## Overview
A high-probability daytrading strategy focusing on 15-minute supply and demand zone reversals, primarily on SPY and QQQ.

## 1. Zone Identification
- **Timeframe:** 15-minute chart.
- **Criteria:** Look for areas of consolidation followed by a strong impulsive move (large body candle).
- **Origin:** Mark the zone at the origin of the imbalance.
- **Freshness:** Fresh zones (untested) have the highest probability of a strong reaction.

## 2. Trading Window
- **Hours:** 9:30 AM – 11:00 AM EST.
- **Why:** Peak market volume and volatility ensure zones react strongly.

## 3. Entry Rules
- **Wait for Price:** Stay patient and wait for price to enter a pre-defined zone.
- **Reversal Only:** Boba looks for price to bounce off demand (LONG) or reject off supply (SHORT).
- **No Chase:** If the zone is missed, wait for the next setup.

## 4. Risk Management (The Boba Way)
- **Max Position Size:** 10% of total account capital.
- **Stop Loss:** 15% (Strict). No exceptions.
- **Risk/Reward:** Target a minimum of 1:2.

## 5. Profit Taking (Scaling out)
- **1st Target (+15%):** Close 25% of position.
- **2nd Target (+20-25%):** Close another 50% of the original position.
- **Runner (+30%+):** Let the remaining 25% run to key historical levels.

## 6. Philosophy
- **Process Over Profits:** Focus on executing the plan perfectly every single time.
- **Patience:** Consistency is harder than one big win.
- **Discipline:** Cut losses fast to protect your "Risk of Ruin."
