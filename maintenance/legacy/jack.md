# Jack's Style & Vibe

## Project Branding: SwjshAK
**Full Name**: Swjsh Army Knife (Algo Precision Tool)
**Vibe**: "Swjsh" is the old gamertag. It represents speed, precision, and that "nothing but net" feeling of a winning trade.
**Aesthetic**: Premium Dark, Glassmorphism, Neon Accents (hsl(var(--brand-primary))), High-Contrast.

## Development Principles
1. **Speed matters**: Live data, real-time sync.
2. **Visual Excellence**: WOW factor at first glance.
3. **Transparency**: Clear journaling and analytics.
