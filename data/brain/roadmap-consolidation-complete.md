## Your New Daily Workflow

### Every Morning (5 minutes)
1. Open [[📅 Daily Log]]
2. Copy template for today
3. Fill in "Today's Goals" (3-5 items)
4. Review [[🎯 Master Tracker]] → "Today's Focus" section

### During the Day
- Update Daily Log as you complete items
- Mark blockers immediately
- Check Master Tracker for context when needed

### Every Evening (5 minutes)
1. Mark completed items with ✅
2. Note any blockers or decisions
3. Write brief reflection (1-2 sentences)

### Every Sunday (15 minutes)
1. Review Daily Log entries from the week
2. Update [[🎯 Master Tracker]] metrics
3. Plan next week's top 3 priorities
4. Archive completed projects
